Crazy, cruel times we're living in when the basic right to not be hungry is being held back by the federal government. Even still, I've been encouraged to see so many people organize to make sure our neighbors don't go hungry, many of whom have been doing the work far before this dark chapter. I've heard of a couple different food distributions and grocery share programs happening, so I'm [compiling them here](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1nS2Y7UDz9TUIf1KhoDexLCr2NNocheQX_c2E6TWauW8/edit?gid=0#gid=0) in case it's helpful to anyone.

- [**Self-Help Hunger Program Distribution:**](https://www.selfhelphunger.org/) Driver Plaza (Adeline x 61st) on Tuesday afternoons
- [**Friday Night Meal:**](https://www.capcoakland.org/friday-night-meal) College Ave Presbyterian Church, 6-7PM
- [**Mandela Food Coop CSA Box:**](https://www.selfhelphunger.org/) $1.50 / box if on SNAP. Pickup Wednesdays at Re-up on College
